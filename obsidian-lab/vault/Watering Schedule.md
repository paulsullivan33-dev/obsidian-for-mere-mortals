# Watering Schedule

| Bed | Water | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North | Deep soak | Twice a week |
| Middle | Light | Every other day |
| South | As needed | Check the soil |

The [[Tomatoes]] sit in the north bed,
so they follow the deep soak row. Pots
by the door are watered by hand every
morning in hot weather; they are not
on this table because they dry out
faster than any rota allows for.

Rain counts. If it rained properly in
the night, skip the day.
