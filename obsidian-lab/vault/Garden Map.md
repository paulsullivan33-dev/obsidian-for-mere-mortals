# Garden Map

The garden is three beds running
downhill, a shed at the top, and a
compost heap in the shaded corner.

## North bed

Tomatoes and peppers. The warmest
strip, against the fence. See
[[Tomatoes]] for what is planted.

## Middle bed

Beans and lettuce, following the
[[Watering Schedule]].

## South bed

Courgettes when there is seed; this
year, resting under a mulch. The
[[Seed Inventory]] explains why.
